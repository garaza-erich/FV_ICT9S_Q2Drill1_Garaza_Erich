Beginner's Guide to Volleyball



Learn the rules, the gear, and the skills you need to start playing.

Basic Rules

  • 6 players on the court
  • Maximum of 3 touches per team
  • First to 25, win by 2
  • Rotate clockwise after winning the serve

Volleyball Equipment

  • Volleyball
  • Knee pads
  • Arm sleeves
  • Volleyball shoes

Basic Skills

  • Service
  • Dig pass
  • Overhead pass
  • Setting
  • Spiking
